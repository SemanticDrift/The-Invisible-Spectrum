## What This Does

One oscillating spacetime field. One equation E = f₀ + (P×A) + Δu. Dark matter, dark energy, and black holes are different frequency bands of the same physics. Visible matter is the mid-range band where all three terms balance. Includes testable predictions for direct detection.

## The Core Insight

A dog whistle produces sound at 23-54 kHz. To you it is silent. Your dog reacts immediately. The sound is not missing. You are just not tuned to that frequency.

The universe is the same. Dark matter, dark energy, and black holes are not mysterious phenomena. They are spacetime oscillating at frequencies outside our detection window.

Light does not travel through space. Light is space oscillating at high frequency. The constant c = 3×10⁸ m/s is not how fast light moves. It is the propagation speed of spacetime oscillations at the visibility threshold.

Different frequency bands produce different parameter combinations in the universal energy equation:

| Phenomenon | f₀ (Hz) | P×A | Δu | Observable As |
|------------|---------|-----|-----|----------------|
| Dark matter | 10³-10⁶ | Negligible | Small | Gravity only |
| Visible light | 10¹⁴-10¹⁵ | Moderate | Atomic | What we see |
| Black holes | >> 10¹⁵ | Extreme | Singular | Invisible (gravity only) |
| Dark energy | >> 10²⁵ | Dominant | Cosmic | Expansion pressure |

One field. One equation. Different parameters. Same physics.
